# Data sources and reuse

The audited snapshot integrates information from:

- [The evolving landscape of drug targets](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-026-01530-3), including its paper-derived approved-drug catalog.
- [Therapeutic Target Database](https://ttd.idrblab.cn/full-data-download), including 2024 target and drug snapshots; see its [2024 resource publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad751).
- [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/api) for selected registry links and study-date candidates.
- [FDA drug databases](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases) for a partially reviewed approval evidence subset.
- [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/help/license), [IUPHAR/BPS Guide to PHARMACOLOGY](https://www.guidetopharmacology.org/about.jsp#license), [ChEMBL](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/), and [NCATS GSRS](https://gsrs.ncats.nih.gov/) for identifier or classification evidence.

The sources have different release dates, jurisdictions, definitions, and coverage. The research database and raw downloads are not bundled here. Public availability does not by itself establish redistribution rights; **TTD's explicit redistribution terms were not independently confirmed**. The committed charts and small summary report derived aggregate counts. Consult each source's own terms before redistributing data or using it commercially. The repository’s MIT license applies to original code, not to third-party data.
