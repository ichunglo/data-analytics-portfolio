Read this before interpreting the results

This is a selected, source-dependent dataset, not a complete matched cohort. No recorded approval does not mean that a drug or target failed. Counts are not clinical success rates. The dashboard contains the 50 highest-ranked targets by linked drug records, so filters and sorting apply only to those 50.

Sources include The evolving landscape of drug targets, TTD, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA, and UniProt. Source snapshots and coverage differ. See source details.
