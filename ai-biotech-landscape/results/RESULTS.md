## What the evidence can and cannot tell us

Some organizations develop their own or in-licensed therapeutics while others supply prediction tools, data, or services. Company and program ownership often differ: licensed assets, regional rights and partner studies should not be merged into a single undifferentiated pipeline. The records retain direct supporting links and explicit unknowns.

Study registration and advancement to a clinical phase do not by themselves establish efficacy, nor show that AI caused an observed outcome. The selection and publication constraints prevent calculating an industry-wide success rate. Historical source dates mean that readers must consult the linked sources for current status.
